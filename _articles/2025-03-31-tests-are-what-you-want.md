Tests Are What You Want, What You Really Really Want - Daniel Hiller, Red Hat & Ryan Hallisey, NVIDIA 

Your project's tests are important, so let's have a conversation and share strategies, insights, and lessons you learned from writing tests for your CNCF project.

Our goal is for maintainers to learn what other projects are doing and inspire new ideas.

Some topics we want maintainers to share:
- Tooling
- Reporting
- Where tests are run
- Dealing with flaky tests
- Performance and scale tests for CRDs and controllers
- Using simulation and using mocks
